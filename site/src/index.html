How it works

Three steps, once.

A small program on your PC makes a list of the folders you choose. The app copies that list to your phone. That's the whole idea.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

  • Instant search, even offline

    Your phone keeps its own copy of the file list, so searching by name or folder is immediate and needs no connection.

  • You choose what's shared

    Pick the folders, switch any off, add your own skip rules, and test whether a path would be shared.

  • Secrets are always skipped

    Passwords, keys, .env files and similar are never listed. Hidden files are off by default.

  • Open and download (optional)

    Preview, open and save files to your phone, with a download queue. Off until you switch it on.

  • Send files to your PC (optional)

    Drop files into one folder you choose, with size and rate limits. Nothing is ever overwritten.

  • Pair with a QR code

    Each phone gets its own key and each code works once. Remove a phone any time.

  • A friendly dashboard

    Manage folders and phones, and fix problems with Check my setup, which says what's wrong and how to fix it.

  • Free and open source

    MIT licensed. No ads, no analytics, no accounts. Read every line on GitHub.

Privacy and security

Your files stay yours.

Nothing is sent to us or to the cloud. There is no server in the middle: the app talks straight to your PC.

Only names travel

By default your phone sees file names, sizes and dates. Contents stay on your PC.

Off by default

Opening files and sending files are separate switches, both off until you turn them on.

You're in control

Per-phone keys, one-time pairing codes, and a button to remove any phone.

Trusted networks only. The link between your phone and PC isn't encrypted yet, so use it on a network you trust, or link just the two devices with a hotspot. Encrypting it is planned.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do my files leave my computer?

Only the names, sizes and dates of the folders you choose are copied to your phone. File contents stay on your PC unless you switch on "Open and download files" and then open or download one. Nothing is ever sent to us or to the internet.

Is the connection encrypted?

Not yet. Your phone and PC talk over plain HTTP on your own network, so use it on a network you trust, or link just the two devices with a hotspot. Encryption is planned. Opening and sending files are off by default for this reason. Read the security model.

Does it work when my PC is asleep?

You can still search and browse the list your phone already has. Updating, opening and downloading need the PC awake and the program running.

Can I use it away from home?

Not directly: the program only accepts connections from your local network, on purpose. A VPN that puts your phone on your home network works if you add its range to allowed_networks.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me about the download?

The builds aren't code-signed yet, which makes both systems cautious about any new program. Check the file against checksums.txt, or build it yourself from the open source code.

My phone can't connect. What now?

Open the dashboard, then Help, Check my setup. It tests your settings, folders, network and firewall and says what to fix. Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, and that no VPN is in the way.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It's free, open source (MIT), with no ads, accounts or analytics.

How do I report a problem?

In the dashboard, Help, Report a problem shows exactly what would be shared (your keys and user name are removed) and lets you copy it. Then open an issue or email {{email}}.
